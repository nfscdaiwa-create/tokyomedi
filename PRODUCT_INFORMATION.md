# Product information provenance

Reviewed: 2026-10-05. The site has 16 medicine records and 21 health-product records.

Medicine indications, administration, contraindications and safety summaries use the specific Japanese PMDA electronic label. Manufacturer links provide product or related company information; some require professional identity confirmation. Wikipedia is background only, never the basis for Japanese regulatory claims. The label date displayed is the revision month printed in the electronic document, not an assumed day.

Health-product ingredient lists, daily amounts and allergens use the exact DHC retail product pages for the catalogued pack. Local photos correspond to the displayed 20/60-day packs. The fish-collagen record uses the brand page and pictured pack; its full nutrition panel, molecular-weight specification and independent laboratory report were not published. These gaps are displayed, not inferred from other products.

All ingredient amounts are per suggested daily serving. Salt/compound weights and active ingredient equivalents must not be conflated. Original Japanese label facts are retained alongside translated names. Product classification follows the current manufacturer page; vitamin E and sustained-release folate have no nutrient-function classification on those pages.
